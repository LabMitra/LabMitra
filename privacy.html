Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

LabMitra books diagnostic tests with home sample collection in Raipur. To do that we need some personal information, including health information. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

1. Who we are

This website, LabMitra, is controlled by BYOS Pathology & Diagnostics, Raipur and operated by Hitendra Lautre ("LabMitra", "we", "us"). BYOS Pathology & Diagnostics is also the laboratory that tests your samples. For the personal data described here, we are the "Data Fiduciary" under the DPDP Act.

3. Why we use it

  • To create and manage your account and bookings.
  • To send our staff to collect the sample at your address and time, and to contact you about the visit (by phone call or WhatsApp).
  • To have your sample tested at our laboratory, BYOS Pathology & Diagnostics. Age and gender are needed so the report shows the correct normal ranges.
  • To give you your report securely online.
  • To take payments, issue receipts and process refunds.
  • To label samples correctly and keep a record of each step, so mistakes can be prevented and investigated.
  • To keep accounting and tax records and meet our other legal obligations.
  • To answer your questions and complaints.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your health information for advertising.

4. Your consent

When you book a test, we ask you to agree to this policy and consent to us using the patient's details and health information to carry out the test and deliver the report. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us (see section 12). Withdrawing consent stops future use, but doesn't affect what we've already done. If you withdraw consent before your sample is tested, we will have to cancel that booking. We may still keep some records where the law requires it (see section 8).

5. Booking for someone else, and children

If you book a test for another person, you confirm that you have their permission to share their details and health information with us. If the patient is under 18, the booking must be made by their parent or lawful guardian, who gives consent on the child's behalf. We do not use children's data for tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising.

6. Who we share it with

We share only what each party needs to do its job:

  • Our laboratory team at BYOS Pathology & Diagnostics uses the patient's name, age, gender, sample ID and tests ordered to carry out the tests and prepare the report.
  • Our sample-collection staff see the visit details: patient name, phone number, address, tests, preparation instructions and amount due.
  • Service providers who run our website for us, under contract and only for these purposes:
    • Supabase: database, logins and secure storage of reports
    • Razorpay: online payments and refunds
    • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google"; our staff also use Google Maps for directions to your address
    • Our SMS provider: to send one-time passwords (OTPs) if you log in with your phone number
    • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): only if you tap "Use current location", to turn your location into an address
    • WhatsApp: only if our staff message you about your visit
  • Government or authorities, when the law requires it, for example reporting certain diseases to health authorities, or responding to a lawful order.

7. Where your data is stored

Our service providers may store and process data on servers outside India. We use reputable providers and take reasonable steps to protect your data wherever it is processed, and we will follow any restrictions the Government of India places on transfers to particular countries.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account details: while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise these details unless the law requires us to keep them.
  • Bookings, lab reports and service records: for as long as needed to provide the service and handle any follow-up or dispute, and for any longer period required by applicable medical-record rules.
  • Payment and invoice records: as required by Indian tax and accounting laws (typically up to 8 years).
  • Saved addresses: until you delete them, or delete your account.

9. How we protect it

  • All connections to our website are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Access controls built into our database mean you can see only your own bookings and reports. Staff see only what they need for collections and reports.
  • Lab reports are kept in private storage. Download links are created only for the account that owns the booking (or our authorised staff) and expire after 2 minutes.
  • Card, UPI and bank details are handled entirely by Razorpay and never reach our servers.

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you can:

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it, and the list of parties we've shared it with.
  • Correct and update: you can edit your name and phone number yourself on the My Account page, or ask us to correct anything else.
  • Erase: ask us to delete your data. We'll do so unless we must keep it for a legal reason (section 8).
  • Withdraw consent: at any time (section 4).
  • Nominate: name someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.
  • Complain: to our Grievance Officer (section 12). If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We'll verify your identity first, and aim to respond within 30 days.

11. Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores a login session so you stay signed in, and on staff devices it remembers the name of the person collecting. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings; you'll then need to log in again.
